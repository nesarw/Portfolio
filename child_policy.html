Quip - Safety Standards & Child Protection Policy

At Quip, we are committed to providing a safe and respectful platform for all users. We have zero tolerance for child exploitation, abuse, or any form of inappropriate content.

1. Commitment to Safety

Quip strictly prohibits any content or behavior that promotes child exploitation, abuse, or harassment. Any violation will result in immediate action, including account suspension and reporting to relevant authorities.

2. Content Moderation

We use AI-powered moderation and manual review to detect and prevent inappropriate content. Users who engage in harmful activities will be permanently banned.

3. User Reporting Mechanism

Users can report any suspicious activity or abuse within the app by using the built-in reporting feature or by contacting our support team at support@quipapp.com.

4. Compliance with Laws

Quip complies with international child protection laws, including the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for minors.

5. Parental Controls & Age Restrictions

Our app is intended for users aged 18 and above. We do not allow underage users and actively remove accounts that violate this policy.

6. Contact & Support

If you have concerns regarding safety on Quip, please contact us at safety@quipapp.com.
